# Bind

Bind is an ability in Hollow Knight: Silksong. It allows Hornet to heal using Silk. It is given to Hornet at the start of the game.

Function
Hornet can heal herself almost instantly by Binding. This move can be performed in the air or on the ground. Binding takes 72 frameshttps://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yCbpMk_tHzU&t=857s

Healing requires 9 Strands of Silk and heals 3 Masks. When Hornet has enough Silk to Bind, the Spool glows brightly.

Modifiers
Various Crests change the way Bind works:

 Reaper Crest 
The Reaper crest changes the bind in a way Hornet can bind normally, except with the change that she can now hit enemies to drop 1-3 silk orbs. Each silk orb gives one silk thread on the spool. This effect lasts approximately 10 seconds.

 Beast Crest 
The Beast Crest reworks Hornet's bind fully, making her enter a fury state, allowing her to heal up to 3 masks from damaging enemies (1 hit = 1 mask gained). This also increases her damage by 0.25x. Although the time this last for is not fully known, we know it is very short.

 Witch Crest 
The Witch Crest makes Hornet not able to heal normally, but instead unleash many tentacles around her, leeching health from enemies. This greatly reduces the frames taken for healing by half, from 72 frames to 36 frames.

 Shaman Crest 
The Shaman Crest forces hornet to  slam into the ground (disabling mid-air binding) and then begin binding for 80 frames (binding frames). If Hornet is too high and binds, when she reaches the ground - she will not regain health and silk is not refunded. 

 Architect Crest 
The Architect Crest does not fully change the bind. It does not change the normal bind but if the player holds up while binding, it will use silk and shell shards to repair tools instead.

Trivia
 It is similar to the Knight's Focus Spell from Hollow Knight in the sense that both are used to heal, and both abilities are automatically given to the characters at the beginning of their games.
