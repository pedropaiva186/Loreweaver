# Caravan

The Caravan are a group of NPCs in Hollow Knight: Silksong teased by Matthew "Leth" Griffin in the Hollow Knight Discord after members were able to solve a riddle.

Little is known about this group except for a description and a gif also provided by the same source.

 "You’re a traveller? A kindred spirit! We hear it's good luck for wanderers to meet on an unfamiliar path. It’s luck we need too, for these roads are haunted and danger snarls at us from every dark corner. Our caravan has only a single, humble wish, but it seems now as though it will never be granted..."

 A friendly band of travellers trekking the dangerous roads of Pharloom in search of something precious. Masters of the journey, their caravan boasts a bounty of flavourful food, powerful drinks and some surprising amenities. They wish to gather the playful young fleas scattered across the kingdom, but this land is far more dangerous than they thought.

Fleas
thumb|right|90px
Fleas are creatures found sleeping throughout Pharloom. When Hornet approaches a Flea it wakes up, lets out a yawn, and then flies off into the foreground - presumably to be taken in by the Caravan. They are described as a "lovely little gentle flea" who have "come to Pharloom for enlightenment". Hollow Knight: Silksong Gameplay - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2019

Getting them all and completing the wish will make a boss, located where the caravan will stay, disappear.

Trivia
 The Caravan is one of a series of NPCs teased using riddles provided by Matthew "Leth" Griffin that lasted throughout June and July of 2020, the others being Huntress, Grindle, and Seth.
 The function of these NPCs seems to be similar to the role Grubfather and the Grubs played in Hollow Knight.
 The riddle solved to reveal these characters featured words that contained some matching letters with the solution. The riddle and solution are outlined below:
 PREPARE THE METHOD
 THE METHOD IS A WORD
 THE WORD HAS 7 LETTERS, OF WHICH:

 CLOTH SHARES 1
 Answer: Letter "C" (Cloth)

 FIREBORN SHARES 3, ONE TWICEThe riddle originally stated "FIREBORN SHARES 2, ONE TWICE", which Leth confirmed to be a mistake.
Answer: Letters "R" and "N" (fiReboRN)

 HIVE SHARES 1
 Answer: Letter "V" (hiVe)

 HORNET SHARES 2
 Answer: Letters "R" and "N" (hoRNet)

 ISELDA SHARES 1
 Answer: Letter "A" (iseldA)

 PALE ORE SHARES 2
 Answer: Letters "A" and "R" (pAle oRe)

 ROYAL WATERWAYS SHARES 5, ONE TWICE
 Answer: Letters "R" and "A" (RoyAl wAteRwAys)

 VESPA SHARES 2
 Answer: Letters "V" and "A" (VespA)

 ZOTE DOES NOT SHARE
 Answer: None

 Final Result: CARAVAN
