# Weathered Mask

The Weathered Mask is a Journal entry in Hollow Knight. It is a mask with a crest that used to belong to the Godseekers in the Land of Storms.Weathered Mask Hunter's Journal entry: "Strange mask from a godless land, passed down over time."

How to Acquire
The Weathered Mask is found within the Land of Storms, a secret area of Godhome. To access this area, all Pantheons need to be completed with all Bindings. The Bindings can be done any number at a time.
