# Clawline (Silksong)

Clawline is an Advanced Weaver talent in the game Hollow Knight: Silksong.

It can be obtained in the Underworks at The Cauldron. The White key is required to acquire enter this area and acquire the talent.

Clawline costs Silk to throw the Silk-strung needle like a harpoon and charge toward it. The harpoon button (default Left Trigger on controller) is used for this talent. 

This talent cannot be used if the character has no silk on the Spool.

The Clawline on a combat perspective can be used to attack enemies doing (on an needle upgraded twice) 6 dmg on when the Clawline hits the enemy and 7 dmg as soon as Hornet finishes the Clawline animation. The Clawline also grants some

invincibility frames although the amount is almost trivial and is usually seen in TAS's.
