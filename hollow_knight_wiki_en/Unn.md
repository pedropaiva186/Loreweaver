# Unn

Unn is an NPC in Hollow Knight who can be found in a pool of acid in the Lake of Unn. She gifts the Shape of Unn Charm.

Lore
Unn is a higher being who is responsible for all life in Greenpath.Greenpath Lore Tablet: "The greater mind once dreamed of leaf and cast these caverns so. In every bush and every vine the mind of Unn reveals itself to us." The temple-like structure at the edge of the Lake of Unn was a place where she was worshipped.Quirrel: "This building suggests some form of worship, though its idol has clearly been long forgotten"Quirrel Dream Nail dialogue: "That lake... a revered presence once slept deep within..." She once existed with land and dream and devotion, though over time her strength faded. Godseeker: "What strength thee once possessed fades beyond time and tune..." Godseeker Dream Nail dialogue: "Sleeping God, to see thee as thou once existed, with land and dream and devotion," Dialogue of the Moss Prophet suggests that the Radiance's light, and the Infection distorts the life Unn created.Moss Prophet: "To stifle that light is to suppress nature. Nature suppressed distorts, plagues us." 

Unn may have at a time long ago been able to manipulate Void to create life, and may have this power still.Bardoon after acquiring Void Heart: "Would it too challenge nature? It could perhaps defeat it." Her power is attuned to creatures of Void, like the Knight.Kingsmould Hunter's Journal entry: "Guard construct of Hallownest's Palace [...] Its heavy white armour protects a fragile body."Wingmould Hunter's Journal entry: "A fly-like construct formed of white shell and a dark liquid interior."Shape of Unn Charm synergy with Baldur Shell and Spore Shroom affect on the Knight The Mosskin were made from the dream of Unn.Greenpath Lore Tablet: "The Green Children walked from the dream unto these lands." Unn is calling to the Mosskin, who are waiting for the call to return.Mosskin and Volatile Mosskin Dream Nail dialogue: "...Unn calls..."Greenpath Lore Tablet: "Here we now shall wait, patient, for the call to return."

In-game events
Unn is located in a pool in the southwest portion of the Lake of Unn. Landing next to this pool will cause her to rise up, stare at the Knight for a moment, and then spit out the Shape of Unn Charm before sinking back into the water.

Unn can be seen in Godhome in the Pantheon of Hallownest as a sleeping mass. Godseeker mentions that she can barely feel Unn's presence anymore.

Location
Unn can be found in a secret room under the Lake of Unn.

Trivia
 It is widely accepted that Unn's gender is female because Moss Knight's Dream Nail dialogue tells us so. However, it is interesting to note that, while Moss Knight's line was not changed, most of the translations refer to Unn's gender as masculine/neuter. The evidence of that is in another line, where Mosskin speak directly to Unn: "Hidden Unn, we need you now." RussianRussian translation: "Сокрытый Унн, ты нужен нам.", FrenchFrench translation: "Unn caché, nous avons besoin de vous maintenant.", SpanishSpanish translation: "Unn, el Oculto, te necesitamos ahora.", PortuguesePortuguese translation: "Unn oculto, precisamos de você agora." translations refer to Unn as masculine/neuterNeed to remember that a lot of Eupopean languages do not distinguish between masculine and neuter genders in their language system.. Of all translations only German one speaks about Unn as a female creatureGerman translation: "Verborgene Unn, wir brauchen dich nun.".
 It is highly possible that, in fact, translators knew the true Unn's gender when they were working on that line but did not change Moss Knight's Dream Nail dialogue either because of a misunderstanding (probably not all of them understood the reference to Unn) or because of unwillingness to correct the English original text.
