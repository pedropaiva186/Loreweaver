# Husk Bully

Husk Bullies are enemies in Hollow Knight. They are walking corpses who attack anyone they can find who has not been Infected.Husk Bully Hunter's Journal entry: "The remains of a bug, animated by a strange force. It aggressively attacks any uninfected creature it finds."

Behaviour and Tactics
Meanders back and forth passively until the Knight comes within range. It then hurls its body a short distance at the Knight. Because of its slow attack sequence and predictable move set, it is very easily dealt with and only poses a threat to the Knight when found in conjunction with other enemies.

Location
Although found mostly in the Forgotten Crossroads, they can also be spotted in the City of Tears, Greenpath, the Howling Cliffs, and the Royal Waterways, much like their hornhead variant.

 Trivia 
 In a Kickstarter update, it was referred to as "Endemons Sapien (Infected)":
 "These vicious, mindless ghouls were once ordinary bugs like you and me. Something below the surface has burnt away their souls and filled them with an aimless rage."Kickstarter update of November 22, 2014.
