# Bellhart

Bellhart is a location in Hollow Knight: Silksong

It resides in the center of Pharloom and marks a halfway point of act 1

By donating Rosaries to the Wish board eventually the welcomer NPC named Pavo will start waving at you violently and if you interact with him he'll thank you and give you the key to an empty Bellhome, entering it reveals it currently only has a bed inside which you can rest on, functioning identically to a bench, except that after sitting down for about 5 seconds Hornet will lay down.

After entering your Bellhome, the Bellhart shop will begin selling cosmetic items for in your Bellhome including a desk, lights, a coat of paint (of a color of your choosing) for the exterior of your Bellhome, a spa (that costs around 1100 rosaries) these are rather expensive and  provide close to no actual benefit except for the spa which will let you regenerate Silk after you buy it by standing in it

you can also find the Materium item in the Memorium area in the Citadel which you can put in the bellhome to see a description of all items, including rosaries and quest items.
