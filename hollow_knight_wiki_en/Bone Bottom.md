# Bone Bottom

Bone Bottom is a small pilgrim camp above the Moss Grotto in the Mosslands of Pharloom.Team Cherry Blog Post of December 13th, 2019. It is the first hub settlement of Hollow Knight: Silksong, similar to Dirtmouth in Hollow Knight. Edge Magazine February 2021 edition, p. 55.

Pilgrims from abroad gather at Bone Bottom in the hopes of gaining entry through the nearby gate to The Marrow, the foundation of Pharloom and the first step in the traditional pilgrimage route to the Citadel.

Little else is known about this area beyond a mention within the reveal trailer that it has a resident named "Grunby of Bonebottom".Hollow Knight: Silksong Reveal Trailer
