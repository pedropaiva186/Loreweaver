# Hunter's Journal (Silksong)

 Hunter's Journal (Silksong) 
The Hunter's Journal is a record of every enemy found in Hollow Knight: Silksong.

 How to obtain 
In order to obtain the Hunter's Journal, you must speak with Nuu in halfway home. If you accept the wish Nuu offers, Bugs of Pharloom, Nuu will give you the Hunter's Journal and ask you to find and kill 100 different creatures. 

NOTE: You do not have to fully fill out each entry entirely, you just need one.

 Use 
The Hunter's Journal is used to record each creature you encounter upon their defeat. After defeating a certain amount of each enemy, amounts may vary, you complete the hunter's notes for that creature. The protagonist Hornet provides her insight into these creatures in the for of Hunter's Notes.

After you collect all 237 Hunter's Notes, if you go to Halfway Home and speak with Nuu she will give you the Hunter's Memento.
