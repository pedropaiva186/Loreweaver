# Cut Content (Hollow Knight)

This page contains a list of cut content for Hollow Knight. Before the release of the game, some content was announced or shown that ultimately did not make it into the final release. Some cut content was never announced but can instead be found in the game's files.

Cut Areas
Most revealed areas did make it into the game, with only minor rooms being scrapped. However, there were some moderately sized areas that were entirely cut from Hollow Knight.
Abyss expansionThere was a Kickstarter stretch goal for an expansion of the Abyss. It was going to be a "massive expansion for only the hardiest of adventurers" with 4 bosses. Since this stretch goal was not reached, the Abyss remained at the size it currently is in the game, without any bosses. The expansion would have "taken players down into the lake of void, to explore the twisted space within."thumb|centerThe Forest of BonesThe Forest of Bones was an area that was originally located in the northeast corner of Hallownest. In the early stages of the game's development, it was planned that three separate areas of the game would each have a Dreamer boss, one of them being the Forest of Bones. The Forgotten Crossroads would connect them together, as well as being the location for the final boss fight.How to design a great Metroidvania map interview with Team Cherry

The Forest of Bones would have had hidden secrets and strange creatures that would have been clad in the bones of dead giants. The lava would have killed the Knight instantly there, and there would have been fifty unique enemies. The Monarch Wings would have been acquired there by fighting a huge bony creature. Throughout the area torches could be lit up and gates could be opened. It would have been the biggest area in the game.

The Forest of Bones was later moved to the bottom of the world, apparently because Ari did not like the idea of a lava area being at the top of the map. Deepnest was then added to connect to the Forest of Bones in its new location.

Later on, this area was scrapped and replaced by the Ancient Basin, while Crystal Peak took its former location. It was ultimately removed due to the constraints its size would have on other areas of Hallownest.The Making of Hollow Knight - Gameinformer interview with Team Cherry.

An area very similar to the Forest of Bones is shown in the Hollow Knight: Silksong trailer. Whether this is the actual Forest of Bones is unknown.thumb|300x300px thumb|300x300pxHoly Grounds / TempleThe Forest of Bones had a sub-area that was announced as the Holy Grounds.Kickstarter update of December 18th, 2014 It was described as a place of worship that was now overtaken. The Holy Grounds had a locked temple gate that could be opened by lighting two huge torches that were guarded by lava worm minibosses. One of the Dreamers, Herrah, could be found in this temple.Reddit AMA answer concerning the Forest of Bones.thumb|300x300pxSnail VillageLittle is known about the Snail Village other than it made an appearance in the A Weird and Wonderful World trailer. Some of its assets can still be found within the game's files. According to Team Cherry, Shelley, the village's only known resident who the Knight is seen interacting with, was just too big and a bit toonier than the final game's art style.Team Cherry's Deviantart post titled "Hollow Knight: Meeting Shelley". Cut Charms
In the reveal of the charms system, an image showing various charms was shown in a Kickstarter update.Kickstarter Update of February 23rd, 2016. Some of these charms made it into the game, although others did not, and some have so far been left unnamed. Several of the cut charms seem to appear on each of the Charm Achievements.

The ability to "Glorify" charms was scheduled to appear in the  Godmaster content pack, when it was still named Gods and Glory.Gods & Glory reveal post. It was cut because Team Cherry did not have the time to add it, and they had difficulty balancing a whole new system for new upgrades.Team Cherry Mixer stream of 27 September 2018.

Cut Dialogue & Developer Notes
The game files of Hollow Knight contain various unused dialogue, notes left by Team Cherry, and other text that cannot be found in the game itself. This text can be extracted from the files with tools such as Asset Studio.

The canonical status of most of this text is debatable as it is not used in the game. However, there is some dialogue that is used in the game which is similar to a few unused lines.

Cut Spells & Abilities
A few spells and abilities that were shown before release were never added to the final game. Some of them were modified and included in Hollow Knight under a different name.
BackdashingBackdashing was an additional movement option that did not make it into the game. It resembles the Dash ability, except in the opposite direction and with a decreased distance.Team Cherry's DeviantArt page showing backdashing.

It was said to have been removed because it "kills the flow of combat more than it adds to it" as well as making no difference in comparison to just immediately dashing in the opposite direction.Messages from lead playtester on Discord.

A value can be found within the game's files called "canBackDash" which would probably test if the Knight has the ability to dash backwards. This value is turned to 0 by default, however, changing it to 1 or any other number won't affect anything in the game.

In the Hollow Knight Beta, it is possible to backdash by pressing the current Super Dash button. If two consecutive backdashes are timed right, the Knight will spin around. A third unique backdash which moves the Knight farther than usual can occur after this if the timing is right.thumb|300x300px|centre
