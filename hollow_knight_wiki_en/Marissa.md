# Marissa

Marissa is a spirit in Hollow Knight. She was a songstress in the Pleasure House in the City of Tears. 

Lore
Marissa was a songstress of some renown in Hallownest's capital.Marissa: "I am Marissa, a songstress of some renown, though given the sorry state of this place, you may find it hard to believe." She performed in the Pleasure House for enrapt crowds, with a voice that was described as being able to ease burdens and still troubled minds.Marissa: "Huge crowds once flocked to hear me sing, then something changed. The audience, once so enrapt, began to leave. I continued to sing yet my voice fell silent upon their ears."City of Tears Lore Tablet: "Songstress Marissa A voice to ease all burdens and still all troubled minds." When the Infection spread across Hallownest, Marissa's audiences eventually stopped coming to her performances. 

At some point, Marissa passed away. Her spirit remained on the stage where she used to perform. She continued to sing here even though nobody was left to listen.Marissa: "I continued to sing yet my voice fell silent upon their ears."

In-game events
thumb|200px|Marissa singing while the Knight listens
If the Knight has the Dream Nail, Marissa can be heard singing on her stage in the Pleasure House. She appears on the stage when the Knight approaches it. After explaining her backstory to the Knight, she asks if they would like to listen to her sing.Marissa: "Perhaps you'd care to listen to me sing? You'll be the first in an age to hear it." 

It is possible for the Knight to sit on the stage to listen to Marissa sing. Even though only the Knight is there to hear her, Marissa enjoys having an audience again.Marissa: "Even if only you, it's a wonderful thing to have an audience again." 

Like all other spirits, the Dream Nail can be used on her, giving 1 Essence.

Location
Marissa is located in the Pleasure House in the City of Tears. A Simple Key is required to unlock the entrance to the Pleasure House.
