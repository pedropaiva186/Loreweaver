# Songclave

 Songclave 

Songclave is a sub-area of the Choral Chambers.

Songclave serves as a town similar to Bone Bottom and Bellhart.

Songclave does have a quest board

It is located on the far top right of Choral Chambers

 White Key 
Songclave also is where you can find the White key which is used to access the right side of The Underworks and The Cauldron

The key can be found on the far right of Songclave on the corpse of a bug. This bug disappears after the key is collected and the town is set up
