# Halfway Home

The "Halfway Home" is a location in the game Hollow Knight: Silksong, found in the Greymoor area It's an inn run by Creige, who offers drinks and rest for a fee. The inn also features other NPCs like Nuu, who gives the player the "Wish Bugs of Pharloom" quest, and the Aged Pilgrim, who rests there briefly.

A bench can be found inside, as well Creige sells a drink of Nectar for  10 Rosaries, which fully heals Hornet and restores 6 Silk.

After fulfilling the Crawbug Clearing Wish for Creige, a giant scarecraw will appear on top of the Halfway Home and the Craws that used to be around will be gone.

As part of the Great Taste of Pharloom Wish, Hornet can pay Creige to allow her to retrieve the Vintage Nectar from the Halfway Home's cellar, which is infested with Skarr
