# Pinmaster Plinney

An NPC in upper Bellhart village after you defeat the Widow.

First Encounter: By claw! Are you then our savior in the shell? Standing so calm and cool on entrance to Plinney's bellhome?

Hornet: What clue, sir, tells of my part in your town's liberation?

Plinney: Why your blade, fierce warrior! Any fine pinmaster would detect the sign of deadly work upon that needle's edge.

T'was a vile curse that struck here. No simple bug, nor basic pin could've granted our release. No!

That blade though, so expertly clutched in claw. That could welly have met the task. It was you who cut us free, yes?

Hornet: I'll grant you've sharp sight, pinmaster, and your saying is fortuitous. This blade that restored your town would now benefit from your service. 

Plinney: Welly, yes! I've a busy backlog of pins to tend, but your gracious heroism decrees that unique weapon receives my care at this very moment! What's say I take a look?

After first sharpening: Bonva! It is done, brave warrior.  By my expert claw, your strange blade has received some fast-sharp shine. It shall cut cleaner than ever before. 

Hornet: Your efforts provide great aid, pinmaster. You have my thanks. 

Upon returning: 

Hornet: Your skills are impressive. My needle sings from your care. But I must ask,  can the blade be honed further? 

Plinney: You seek further sharpening? Further than Plinney does provide? Impertinence! But... maybe? But... No...

Hornet: You hesitate? Then it is possible? Are you incapable? Should I seek others within the kingdom?

Plinney: Wait! Yes. Seek not others, but an oil, fabled and fine.  With it the Citadel once brought gleaming edge and point to its pins.

Though the Citadel's pinmasters are long past, some small store of that miraculous elixir may remain within its halls.

Were even a mere drop pilfered, and gifted upon Plinney, your blade could shine brighter still...

Just know, dear friend, the veiled bugs would be oh so unkind to the thief who'd claim that fine prize for themself. 

Upon accepting Pinmaster's Wish:

I will find the oil, pinmaster. My needle has seen much use in this cruel kingdom, and I suspect that many more battles are due. I will value your further skills upon my blade.

Plinney: It is a dangerous wish you accept of this poor pinmaster. I welly hope this one's professional desire does not cost you your life.
