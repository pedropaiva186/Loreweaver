# Bees

The lands of Hallownest are filled by several groups of strange sentient creatures. They occupy various territories around the vast kingdom and developed their own societies and cultures aside from the Pale King's rule.

The residents of the Hive, below the Kingdom's Edge, are a species of sentient bees.Wanderer's Journal, p. 118. These airborne warriors possess a hive mind which dictates their behaviour among the Hive. Hivelings carry out simple tasks,Hiveling Hunter's Journal entry: "Carries out simple tasks." and can colonise husks to serve the Hive's purpose.Husk Hive Hunter's Journal entry: "Cowardly husk, its body colonised by hivelings." Soldiers and Guardians defend the Hive against intruders. Lastly, the flightless but fierce  Hive Knight acts as the last rampart before their monarch,Hive Knight Hunter's Journal entry: "Loyal protector of the Hive's Queen." Hive Queen Vespa, who grew so huge she could not leave the Hive.Official manual: "ruled by a queen who grew so huge she could no longer leave her domain."

The Hive attacks any intruder who does not wield their blood. They also rejected the Pale King's attempt at perpetuation and separated themselves from the kingdom of Hallownest.Hive Queen Vespa: "My Knight... At last you are freed." "Though this hive exists within Hallownest, we play no part in its attempt at perpetuation." Despite their isolation, Vespa's death weakened the Hive, allowing the Infection to spread through.AMA answer concerning the Hive on Reddit The Infected residents never realised the death of their queen. Their corrupted hive mind dictated to defend her tirelessly, much to the sadness of their queen's spirit.
