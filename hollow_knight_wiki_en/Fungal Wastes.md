# Fungal Wastes

The Fungal Wastes are a humid area located near the middle of Hallownest with connections to several other areas.

Description
The noxious caverns of the Fungal Wastes are covered in mushrooms of all kinds.Cornifer: "I'm just about finished charting these noxious caverns..." Besides this, caustic pools of acid are abundant throughout the area.

The Fungal Wastes are populated by the Mushroom Clan, a territorial group who pose a threat to anyone who wanders nearby.Cornifer: "Very territorial types make their homes within this area."Wanderer's Journal, p. 55. The Mantis Tribe make their home further into the area and are equally unwelcoming to outsiders.Cloth: "A long necked critter warned me of a tribe deeper down. Warrior sorts, so she says and I'm itching for some serious combat." One of the main features of the area is the large Stag Station in the west part, Queen's Station.

thumb|left|200px|Typical room in the Fungal Wastes
Various NPCs are present throughout the area. Leg Eater has a shop where he sells Fragile Charms in the northeast part of the Fungal Wastes. Near his shop, Cloth can be found hiding under the ground. Quirrel can be encountered twice in the area, once in the Stag Station and later in Mantis Village. In the very south of the area, near Mantis Village, Bretta can be freed and returned to Dirtmouth. The Warrior Dream Elder Hu is found in the northeast near the entrance to the City of Tears. In the Mantis Village, the Mantis Lords can be challenged to gain access to an entrance to Deepnest. 

A Charm Notch can be picked up after defeating two Shrumal Ogres in the north part of the Fungal Wastes. Three Charms are found throughout the area, Dashmaster in the southeast, Spore Shroom in the southwest near Deepnest, and Mark of Pride in a chest in Mantis Village after the Mantis Lords are defeated. After picking up the Mantis Claw from Mantis Village, a Mask Shard can be acquired in Queen's Station.

Lore
The native population of the Fungal Wastes mainly consists of the Mushroom Clan and the Mantis Tribe. The Mushroom Clan is a group of fungal creatures who have a shared mind.Fungal Wastes Lore Tablet: "Strength in the shared self. Strength in the mind united." They share a border with Deepnest, whose inhabitants they were in conflict with.Fungal Wastes Lore Tablet: "This border bounds the twisting, scratching things. [...] No peace with them we make." When the Pale King arrived in Hallownest, they accepted his rule due to their strong belief that his foresight could protect them.Fungal Wastes Lore Tablet: "Warily shall we accept the will of the Wyrm. Its prescience shields us." 

However, the Mantis Tribe who live in the south part of the area had no love for the kingdom of Hallownest.Mantis Lords Hunter's Journal entry: "The Mantis tribe and the bugs of old Hallownest had no love for each other." The Mantises agreed to a truce where they would retain sovereignty over their territory, and in return, they would keep the beasts of Deepnest at bay.Mantis Village Lore Tablet: "The truce remains. Our vigil holds. The beasts are kept at bay."Wanderer's Journal, p. 61. 

Queen's Station|200px|thumb
The Fungal Wastes was an important area for travellers. The Pilgrim's Way, a road leading from the Forgotten Crossroads to the City of Tears, passed through the caverns.Forgotten Crossroads Lore Tablet: "The Pilgrim's Way. Travellers of Hallownest, descend through verdant wilds and fungal groves to the city at this kingdom's heart." Queen's Station connected Fog Canyon and the Fungal Wastes to the Stagways, becoming a hub of travel for travellers who wanted to visit the outer edges of Hallownest.Last Stag: "This place was a bustling hub of travel, well-placed for travellers visiting the outer edges of the land."

When the Infection spread across the land, the Mushroom Clan became Infected and started becoming aggressive to outsiders. While the Mantis Tribe did not fall to the Infection, they retained their tradition of attacking anyone who wandered near their village.Quirrel: "The sickness in the air that clouds the mind of lesser beasts... they resist it. They retain their intellect and their honour, though also their lethal traditions."

How to access
Entrance from Fog Canyon|200px|thumb
The Fungal Wastes can be accessed from the south part of Fog Canyon, which connects to Queen's Station. To progress further into the area, the Mothwing Cloak is required. Otherwise it can be accessed by using 3 Vengeful Spirits, which is commonly used by speedrunnershttps://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qz5OiIqg9Qg Clip of Queen's Station skip.. 

Another way to first enter the area is from the southwest part of the Forgotten Crossroads, near the Hot Spring. The Mothwing Cloak is also required to enter this way.

Sub-area: Queen's Station

Queen's Station is a run-down Stag Station which serves as a connection between the Fungal Wastes and Fog Canyon. In Hallownest's prime, Queen's Station was a bustling hub of travel, said to have been one of the busiest stations in the kingdom.Wanderer's Journal, p. 57. Even though the station is decayed, it is still possible to hear the ringing of the bells and the voices of bugs.Quirrel: "Is it that, just faintly, you can still hear the echo of the bells?"Wanderer's Journal, p. 56.

Willoh can be found in one of the Stagway tunnels where she is eating fungus.Willoh: "I chanced upon a unique little fungus growing right above us." Quirrel can be encountered in the main hub after arriving from Fog Canyon for the first time. The east part of Queen's Station contains a Mask Shard which can be acquired after getting the Mantis Claw. 

Sub-area: Mantis Village

Mantis Village is the home of the Mantis Tribe, a group of mantises who are able to resist the Infection. They have a rich history, and their culture predates the birth of the kingdom of Hallownest.Wanderer's Journal, p. 60. The border of their village contains heads of beasts mounted on poles to deter any wanderers. Since the mantises shunned technology brought about by the Pale King, they made their buildings from sinew and bone.

While initially hostile, the mantises become neutral after the Mantis Lords are defeated.

The Mantis Claw ability can be picked up in the west part of the village. Quirrel can be found near the entrance of the main village when the area with the Mantis Lords is entered for the first time. After the Mantis Lords' defeat, the Mark of Pride Charm can be found in a chest in the southeast. Their defeat also opens up a connection to Deepnest.

Sub-area: Fungal Core

Fungal Core is a small area with several hidden tunnels which is accessible after acquiring the Monarch Wings. Its main features are a Lore Tablet which can be read with the Spore Shroom Charm equipped, and a big mushroom corpse which can be Dream Nailed. 

The bottom of the zone leads to a hidden Mask Shard in Deepnest. 

Trivia
 It is possible to stand on the purple mushrooms by using the Desolate Dive/Descending Dark Spell or the Cyclone Slash Nail Art while above them.
 This is also possible through a frame-perfect usage of the  Mothwing Cloak.
 There are several Lore Tablets in the Fungal Wastes, but the Spore Shroom Charm is required to read them.
 While wearing the Spore Shroom Charm near a Lore Tablet, the background music becomes quiet and a chorus of voices speaking the same gibberish babbling of Mister Mushroom can be heard.
