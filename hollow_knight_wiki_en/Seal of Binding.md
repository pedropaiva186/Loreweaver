# Seal of Binding

The Seal of Binding is a Journal entry in Hollow Knight. It is a seal that is used to contain something powerful or preserve something important.Seal of Binding Hunter's Journal entry: "Used to contain a powerful force, or to preserve something of great importance."

How to Acquire
The Seal of Binding Journal entry is acquired after conquering the Path of Pain.
