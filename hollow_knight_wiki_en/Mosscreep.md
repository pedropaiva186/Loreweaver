# Mosscreep

Mosscreeps are enemies in Hollow Knight. They camouflage themselves with leaves and hide from danger amongst the undergrowth.Wanderer's Journal, p. 44.Mosscreep Hunter's Journal entry: "Weakling that covers itself in leaves to appear larger. Hides amongst the undergrowth."

Behaviour and Tactics
Hides in greenery until the Knight approaches. Once approached they shuffle about in their bush before rising up and wandering back and forth.

Location
They are found in abundance in Greenpath and the Queen's Gardens.

Trivia
 The Mosscreep is one of the few enemies that respawns upon leaving and re-entering the room.
 There is a glitch where, if the Knight opens the Map while a Mosscreep is moving upside-down towards the edge of a platform, it continues moving, off the platform and through the air.
