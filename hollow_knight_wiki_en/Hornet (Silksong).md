# Hornet (Silksong)

Hornet is the mysterious princess-protector of Hallownest’s ruins, the deuteragonist of Hollow Knight and the main protagonist of Hollow Knight: Silksong. She wields a Needle and thread with deadly prowess.Official manual 

Hornet is captured by outsider bugs and brought to their kingdom, Pharloom, but she fortunately escapes her cage before arriving at their destination. 

Health and Silk
Health
Health is represented as Masks, shown in the upper-left corner of the screen. Hornet starts the game with 5 Masks and can obtain more. Health is lost by taking damage, either from enemies or from environmental hazards. Health can be regained by the use of the Bind ability consuming a full Silk spool.

Lifeblood Masks, granted by Lifeblood needles or Lifeblood cocoon, are temporary masks.

Silk
Silk is represented as a spool below the health indicator. Thread is consumed when using special abilities, including a form of Focus named Bind. Striking enemies with the Needle fills the spool by one lace, up to a total of nine. Other effects (such as from tools) may fill laces partially, with different style of filling up lace. When the spool is full, the symbol representing the side of the spool left to the health indicator is filled, indicating a Bind is possible.

Weapon and Tools

Needle
The Needle is Hornet's main weapon. Not only can she use it to slash or block attacks against enemies, but also to dash forward, stab, and bounce up above them. She can also use it alongside her abilities to deal greater damage or as a grappling hook.
Crests
Crests are items that act effectively like a character class template. Each one alters Hornet's move set, attack patterns, Bind skill and the allowed spread of Tools that Hornet can equip; Hornet starts with the Hunter Crest. Crests can be changed when sitting on a Bench similar to Charms.
Tools
Tools are special items classed by categories, equipped on a Crest with limited slots. They can be used as throwing weapons or defensive aids without costing Silk. Tools can be changed when sitting on a Bench similar to Charms.

Abilities
Abilities mentioned don't consume Silk unless specified so.

 Swift Step 
Swift Step is a Fabled Weaver talent.

This talent lets you dash forward and sprint with unnatural speed, as though gliding upon silk.

You can perform a dive dash to land quicker.

 Cling Grip 
Cling Grip is an Ancient Weaver technique.

This talent allows you to cling to sheer surfaces and leap off to rapidly scale heights.

Pressing dash prompt allows you to climb a short distance and then jump if you reach edge.

 Needolin 
Needolin is a Holy Weaver talent.

Needolin allows you to Play ancient melodies by stringing Silk along the needle. 

Needolin uses Silk thread at a speed of 10 seconds a lace. 

The Needolin is used in various places across Pharloom to uncover main content as well as secrets.

 Clawline 
Clawline is an Advanced Weaver talent. It can be obtained in the Underworks at The Cauldron. The White key is required to acquire enter this area and acquire the talent.

This talent costs Silk to throw the Silk-strung needle like a harpoon and charge toward it. It cannot be used if the character has no silk on the Spool.
