# Needle

thumb|Hornet's Needle
Needle is the term used to describe the bladed weapon wielded by Hornet, attached with a thread of Silk.

 Upgrades 
The Needle can be upgraded by Pinmaster Plinney up to four times. Each upgrade adds four additional damage.
