# Pharloom

Pharloom is the location where Hollow Knight: Silksong takes place. 

The kingdom of Pharloom is located in a distant and withered land.Official website: "Captured and Taken to a Distant Land. Hornet, princess-protector of Hallownest, finds herself alone in a vast, unfamiliar world." It is ruled and haunted by silk and song, the two powerful forces of that world.Blog post of March 19, 2019: "Hornet will wake in this withered land, a kingdom dedicated to faith and pilgrimage, crowned by an enormous, shining citadel. [...] Lace: A lithe fencer wielding a gilded pin, the traditional weapon of Pharloom."Blog post of February 14th, 2019: "[...] adventure through a whole new kingdom haunted by silk and song."Hollow Knight: Silksong - Details from Team Cherry Like Hallownest in its golden age, Pharloom is dedicated to faith and pilgrimage, although the gilded pin is its traditional weapon and the silk spool its symbol. Pharloom is crowned by the shining Citadel, the final destination for all pilgrims, and the place where all the secrets of the haunting lie.

Pharloom's Folly
Pharloom's Folly is a text, part of which is shown during the opening sequence of Silksong.
They see your beauty, so frail and fine,

They see your peace, woven of faith and toil,

They forget your heart, bound in slumber and servitude,

When you wake they shall see your truth,

A beast's nature bare to all.

- From 'Pharloom’s Folly' by the Conductor Romino Hollow Knight: Silksong Gameplay - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2019
