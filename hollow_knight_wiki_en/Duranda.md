# Duranda

Durandas are enemies in Hollow Knight. They are large flying creatures who are protected by their spikey shell, largely ignoring outsiders.Duranda Hunter's Journal entry: "Simple flying creature protected by a spikey shell."Wanderer's Journal, p. 46.

Behaviour and Tactics
Floats around passively, always facing the Knight. It only takes damage from its unarmoured front and can be used to traverse areas that are above thorns before acquiring Crystal Heart by Nail-bouncing on its hard shell.

Location
They can only be found in Greenpath, serving as obstacles for reaching Nailmaster Sheo's tent.

Trivia
 Its shell has a separate health value of 9999, though it cannot be damaged.
