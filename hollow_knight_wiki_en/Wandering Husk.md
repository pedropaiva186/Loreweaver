# Wandering Husk

Wandering Husks are enemies in Hollow Knight. Once citizens of Hallownest, these reanimated corpses now wander the roads of the kingdom.Wandering Husk Hunter's Journal entry: "The remains of a bug, animated by a strange force. Wanders the roads where it once lived."

Behaviour and Tactics
Meanders back and forth until disturbed. It then flails its arms about wildly and runs at the Knight, dealing contact damage.

Location
They can be found in large quantities in the Forgotten Crossroads, though only one can be found there after they become Infected. They can also be found in Greenpath, the City of Tears, and Deepnest.

Trivia
 In a Kickstarter update, it was referred to as "Endemons Sapien (Infected)":
"These vicious, mindless ghouls were once ordinary bugs like you and me. Something below the surface has burnt away their souls and filled them with an aimless rage."Kickstarter update of November 22, 2014.
