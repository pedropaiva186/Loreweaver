# Willoh

Willoh is an NPC in Hollow Knight. She eats fungus in a broken-down Stag Station in Queen's Station.

Lore
Willoh is a giraffe weevilReddit AMA comment on character design. who found a unique fungus growing in Queen's Station. Since she is wary of the dangers of the Fungal Wastes and Fog Canyon, she wants to stay in Queen's Station for as long as her food lasts.Willoh: "I chanced upon a unique little fungus growing right above us." "This place must once have been quite the hub, built at the border between two lands, that misty drop to one side and those bulbous growths another. Neither place seems particularly welcoming so while my little treats last, I think I'll stay right here."

Besides the fungus in the station, Willoh has eaten various bugs and thinks about eating the Knight as well.Willoh Dream Nail dialogue: "This little creature looks tasty. I wonder, should I eat it? The others around here were awfully bland."

Willoh met Cloth at some point, warning her about the Mantis Tribe in Mantis Village.

In-game events
Willoh can be found in Queen's Station in the room above the functioning Stag Station.

It is possible to reach the area where Willoh is eating fungus by using the Monarch Wings. A corpse on the platform reveals, when struck with the Dream Nail, the following dialogue:
 "...Please ...Not food..."

This platform also contains a Hallownest Seal.

Location
Willoh is located in Queen's Station above the functional Stag Station.
