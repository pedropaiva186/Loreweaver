# Hollow Knight First Chapter: Quirrel

Hollow Knight First Chapter: Quirrel is the first chapter of the Hollow Knight comic series.     

 Plot 
Quirrel is walking through a wasteland outside of Hallownest. While inspecting the shells of smaller bugs, he hears Boon grunting as they're getting attacked by vicious bugs. Quirrel uses his nail to cut down the bugs. Boon cooks soup while they shelter from the wind. And after they eat, they travel together. After following a hidden path, they part ways and Quirrel finds a cave full of bugs emitting light. Quirrel rests and goes to sleep, dreaming of The Forgotten Crossroads, Fog Canyon, City of Tears, The Temple of the Black Egg and The Hollow Knight. Quirrel wakes up and begins traversing the terrain once more. He finally comes across a giant mountain, with Hornet staring at him from above. Hornet tries to convince Quirrel to turn back, but he stands his ground. Hornet throws her needle at Quirrel and they begin dueling. After striking Quirrel's mask, it emits a white glow. Hornet recognizes the mask and flees back up the mountain, letting Quirrel enter Hallownest. Quirrel soon finds himself on a path leading to Dirtmouth.
