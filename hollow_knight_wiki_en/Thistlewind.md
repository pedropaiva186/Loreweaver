# Thistlewind

Thistlewind is a moth spirit in Hollow Knight. They give information on the Moth Tribe and on Markoth.

Lore
Thistlewind was an explorer who travelled across Hallownest.Thistlewind: "I've been many places and done a great many things, but I suspect you will outdo me by far. I've long ceased travelling the caverns of Hallownest and I suspect they're now far more dangerous than in my journeys, though with your capable air I'm sure you'll do fine." Despite the moths being a passive species, they were one of the few moths who wielded a weapon.Thistlewind: "You find it strange to see a moth wielding a weapon? We are a passive species, though I am not the only one amongst us to take up arms." Eventually, Thistlewind died and was buried in Spirits' Glade.

In-game events
Thistlewind appears above their grave in Spirits' Glade when the Knight approaches. They explain that they have stopped travelling Hallownest and suspect that it has become a much more dangerous place. Thistlewind wonders what became of Markoth, who went to Kingdom's Edge to uncover a truth long forgotten.Thistlewind: "Another braved the edges of this world, hoping to uncover a truth long forgotten. I wonder what became of him?"

Like all other spirits, the Dream Nail can be used on them, giving 1 Essence.  This causes Revek to attack.

Location
Thistlewind is located in Spirits' Glade in the Resting Grounds.
