# Gluttonous Husk

Gluttonous Husks are enemies in Hollow Knight. In life, these upper-class bugs consumed so much food that they took on a grotesque shape.Gluttonous Husk Hunter's Journal entry: "Former member of Hallownest's high society. Uses its corpulent body as a weapon." "[...] they hunted less and consumed more, eventually taking on a grotesque shape [...]." Reanimated after death, they now use their body to attack.Emilitia: "That's them outside, their bodies shambling around all mindless and empty. And I'm still alive to witness their pathetic demise."

Behaviour and Tactics
Meanders back and forth until disturbed. When engaged, it hops a short distance in the Knight's direction, dealing contact damage.

Location
They are mainly found in the east part of the City of Tears, much like the Cowardly Husk and Husk Dandy.
