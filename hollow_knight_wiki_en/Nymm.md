# Nymm

Nymm is an NPC in Hollow Knight. He gives the Carefree Melody Charm to the Knight.

Lore
Nymm is a bug who arrives in Dirtmouth after the Grimm Troupe is banished. He plays an accordion-like bug to bring cheer to the fading town, although he admits that even his song is a little on the sadder side.Nymm: "My dear friend, are you enjoying my music? It's as upbeat a tune as I know, but I must admit, even it falls a little on the sadder side." Nymm is content to be in Dirtmouth beside the company of the Knight.Nymm: "Being in this town, beside such good company as yourself, I feel remarkably, refreshingly content."

He greatly resembles Brumm without his mask. Nymm has no memory of why he travelled to Dirtmouth, or where he came from.Nymm: "Why did I choose to travel here? And how far must I have come... to forget the origin entirely." He is not sure how he obtained Carefree Melody, but when he looks at its design it makes him think of something and somebody that he lost and forgot about.Nymm: "To be honest, I'm not even sure where I came upon it, but staring at its design, my memory stirs, something, and someone, forgotten, lost far away. The specifics elude me, but a strange sense remains, fear, but also... longing? Like a strange nostalgia for something that never happened."

In-game events
If the Grimm Troupe is banished from Hallownest, Nymm appears in Dirtmouth beside the Bench. If the Ritual is completed by defeating Nightmare King Grimm, Nymm does not appear. 

Nymm gives the Knight the Carefree Melody Charm when he is listened to.

Location
Nymm is located in Dirtmouth, next to the Bench.

Trivia
 The music Nymm plays, Accordion Nymm, has the same melody as the Dirtmouth theme.
