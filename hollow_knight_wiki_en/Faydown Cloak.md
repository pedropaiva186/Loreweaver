# Faydown Cloak

The Faydown Cloak is an ability in Hollow Knight: Silksong. The Faydown Cloak provides Hornet with a double jump ability, similar to the Monarch Wings from Hollow Knight. It has a secondary ability which keeps the user from freezing, though it does not prevent damage from touching the water in Mount Faye. The Drifter's Cloak can't be used while the double jump is available.

 Location 
The Faydown Cloak is acquired by reaching the top of Mount Faye.  Before attempting to climb Mount Faye, Hornet must have the Clawline, Cling Grip, and Swift Step abilities. The player also needs a lot of speed and skill to reach the top of Mount Faye, as the cold air begins to damage you after a set amount of time. The only way to reset the timer besides leaving Mount Faye is to find lanterns which warm Hornet up. These lanterns are relatively spread out across Mount Faye, so the player has to move very quickly at several points. After the Cloak has been acquired, Hornet will no longer be able to be frozen and thus has no more need for lanterns.
