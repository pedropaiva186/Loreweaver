# Brumm

Brumm is an NPC in Hollow Knight. He is involved in the quest of the Grimm Troupe.

Lore
Brumm is a member of the Grimm Troupe. Although he initially encourages the Knight to search for flames for the Grimmchild, he begins having second thoughts about the Ritual later on.

He does not approve of the Ritual playing itself out repeatedly and endlessly,Brumm: "Endless, repeating songs of sacrifice, of servitude. For the Ritual. For the troupe. For the Master." "A song that never ends... is no song at all." considering himself and the rest of the Troupe slaves to it.Brumm: "Endless, repeating songs of sacrifice, of servitude. For the Ritual. For the troupe. For the Master. Even this child was born into invisible chains." "Master... are even you a slave? If so, let our chains be broken together!" Brumm believes the gathering of flames desecrates the dead Kingdom of Hallownest.Brumm: "Perhaps together we can banish that livid flame and let this dead Kingdom rest in peace." "It is painful to defy the Master, but our harvest... it profanes this dark, quiet Kingdom." He wants to make the Ritual fail by destroying the Nightmare Lantern, the anchor keeping the Grimm Troupe in Hallownest.Brumm: "This once, I would see the Ritual fail. Mrmm.. Now! Let us destroy the anchor, and banish the Master."

In-game events
When the Grimm Troupe is first summoned, he can be found playing the accordion just inside of Grimm's tent in Dirtmouth.

After Grimm has been defeated, Brumm appears in Distant Village where he gives the Knight a Flame if they do not already have three Flames. He then offers to help the Knight banish the Grimm Troupe. Should the Knight complete the Ritual instead, he explains that he bears them no hatred.Brumm: "But if you wish to continue the Ritual... Mrmm. As long as you do it without regret, I will bear you no hatred."

Brumm is next found in the Howling Cliffs in the room of the Nightmare Lantern, where he jabs his staff into the brazier so it can be broken.

If the Grimm Troupe is banished, Brumm disappears, and an amnesic bug named Nymm appears in Dirtmouth who greatly resembles him.

Location
Brumm appears in three locations depending on how far the Grimm Troupe's quest has progressed.

Trivia
 Brumm's accordion music in Dirtmouth can also be heard while standing outside the tent.
 The accordion that Brumm plays has limbs, implying that it is a dead or live bug.
 Brumm's name within the game's files is "IGOR" a possible reference to the assistant of Dr. Frankenstein in the Universal Pictures adaptations of the novel Frankenstein.
