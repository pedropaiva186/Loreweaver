# Knight

The Knight is the protagonist and only playable character of Hollow Knight.

 Hollow Knight Silksong 
In Hollow Knight: Silksong, the Knight appears before Hornet during the true ending, Sister of the Void. After the power of the Everbloom fails, Hornet and Lace are saved from being claimed by the tendrils of the Void by the Knight, who attacks the tendrils in retaliation. When Hornet awakes to find their sibling leading the army of the countless fallen Vessels having returned to the Void, she sees glimpses of their ascended collective form, the Void Given Focus. 

She loses consciousness once more, but her sibling uses multiple tendrils of the Void to lift her and Lace out of the Abyss and back onto the dry land below Pharloom, where the webs of the Abyss have started to fade away from the greater kingdom above. After that, the Vessels once more return to the Abyss.

In-game events
At the beginning of the game, the Knight is equipped with only its Old Nail and the ability to heal itself using SOUL. Throughout the game, the Knight gains access to new powers, Items, and Charms to help it through its journey.

After encountering the Dreamers in the Resting Grounds, the Knight acquires the Dream Nail. With this weapon, it can break the Seals in the Temple of the Black Egg by killing each of the three Dreamers. This allows it to enter the Black Egg, where it can fight the Hollow Knight. If the Hollow Knight is faced before obtaining Void Heart Charm, the Knight kills its sibling and takes its place in sealing the Radiance.

thumb|200px
If the Knight has fought Hornet twice and has the King's Brand and the Shade Cloak, it has access to both halves of the Kingsoul. When the complete Kingsoul is equipped, the Knight can transform this Charm into Void Heart by going to its birthplace at the bottom of the Abyss and remembering Its past. Void Heart allows the Knight to unify the Void under its will.Void Heart description: "Unifies the void under the bearer's will."

With Void Heart, the Knight can use the Dream Nail on the Hollow Knight after Hornet holds it down. If the Dream Nail is not used, the Knight has to kill the Hollow Knight. This gives a similar ending as the ending without having Void Heart, but instead, Hornet becomes a Dreamer, her face appearing on the entrance to Black Egg.

thumb|200px
Using the Dream Nail initiates the fight with the Radiance. At the end of this fight, the Knight breaks its own shell and lands the finishing blows on the Radiance in its Shade form, with the help of the Shade of the Hollow Knight. Both siblings and all the other Shades in the Abyss then return to the Void.

In Godhome, the Knight can ascend by attuning all the Gods of Hallownest and defeating them in the Pantheons. Godtuner pick up text: "Seek the gods of Hallownest. Tune their power. Through their strength, ascend." In the Pantheon of Hallownest, at the end of the fight with Absolute Radiance, the Knight breaks its shell and its Shade falls into the Void. The Void Entity then appears, killing Absolute Radiance, after which Void starts leaking from the Godseeker's body in the Junk Pit and eventually bursts out of her. If Godseeker was given a Delicate Flower, the Void and Godseeker disappear instead.

Health and SOUL
Health

center|link=|36x36px
Health is represented as Masks, shown in the upper-left corner of the screen. The Knight starts the game with 5 Masks, and can obtain more by collecting Mask Shards. 4 Mask Shards make one new Mask of health, up to a max of 9 total Masks of health. Health is lost by taking damage, either from enemies or from environmental hazards. Health can be regained by resting at a Bench or Hot Spring, by Focusing or by obtaining a Mask.
center|link=|48x48px
Lifeblood masks, granted by Lifeseeds and certain Charms, are temporary Masks that cannot be healed using Focus. Lifeblood Masks disappear upon resting at a bench, whereas those granted by Charms regenerate.

Shade

link=Shade|center|48x48px
When Masks are depleted to 0, the Knight's shell shatters which releases its Shade. After the screen fades back in from black, the Knight is back on the Bench that was last rested on with a third of its SOUL gauge missing and without any Geo. Returning to the area where the Knight died and defeating its Shade restores the lost SOUL gauge and recovers the Geo that was lost. However, if its masks have been depleted to zero without first defeating its Shade, its previously lost Geo cannot be recovered.

SOUL

link=Soul|center|48x48px
SOUL is represented as a circular face to the left of the health indicator. SOUL is used to cast Spells, including Focus. Striking enemies with the Nail is the primary source of regaining SOUL, though other tools and objects also grant SOUL. Collecting three Vessel Fragments increases the maximum SOUL by 33.

Upgrades and Equipment
Nail

center|100x100px|link=Nail
The Knight's main weapon, able to swing in any direction. The Nail can be swung downwards while the Knight is above spikes, enemies, or certain objects, which can be a useful tool for traversing Hallownest. The Nail can be upgraded for stronger strikes, and charged attacks called Nail Arts can be learned from the Nailmasters.

Charms

center|100x100px|link=Charms|class=darklight-img
Special trinkets acquired through adventuring or by purchasing from other bugs. By equipping It on available Notches, Charms enhance the Knight's power through a variety of different methods, including increasing health, reducing heal times, spawning allied hatchlings, and more.

Spells

center|100x100px|link=Spells|class=darklight-img
First acquired from the Snail Shaman, these magical attacks deal large amounts of damage in short bursts, at the cost of SOUL. Each Spell can be upgraded to a stronger version by finding hidden rooms throughout Hallownest.

Abilities

center|100x60px|link=Spells and Abilities (Hollow Knight)
Skills and tools found throughout Hallownest that allow the Knight to more effectively traverse the terrain and engage enemies in combat, including dashing, wall-jumping, Super-dashing, and double-jumping.

Trivia
 The Knight's design is taken from Team Cherry's first game, Hungry Knight, which was made during a Game Jam that started the company.
 While the two games are very different, Team Cherry has stated that they "liked the main character and the general aesthetic so much that [they] fleshed it out and expanded on it."Team Cherry's list of game jam games.
 This plan was put forward through the conceptual "Hungry Bug" artworks done by Ari Gibson, which helped visualise and shape the world of Hollow Knight.Team Cherry's DeviantArt profile.
 Mister Mushroom makes a reference to Hungry Knight in his first dialogue with It, saying "...It's nice that you were able to help your friend. So you're not hungry any more?"
