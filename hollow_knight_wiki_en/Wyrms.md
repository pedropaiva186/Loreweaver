# Wyrms

Wyrms were ancient, powerful beings who "pulled bugs into their thrall".Bardoon: "This ashen place is grave of Wyrm. Once told, it came to die. But what is death for that ancient being? More transformation methinks. This failed kingdom is product of the being spawned from that event."Mister Mushroom: "Wyrms pull bugs into their thrall," They were larger than even Bardoon, though they had fewer limbs. They also had the ability of foresight, meaning that they could predict the future to some extent.Bardoon: "Don't mistake. I am not a wyrm... Ohrm... Too small, I am. Too many limbs. No foresight like those old things." One of these Wyrms died in Kingdom's Edge, transforming into the Pale King. A Lore Tablet in the Fungal Wastes remarks on him having prescience as well.Fungal Wastes Lore Tablet: "Warily shall we accept the will of the Wyrm. Its prescience shields us."

There is a reference to another being called the Blackwyrm in White Defender's journal entry.White Defender Hunter's Journal entry: "The Champion's Call, the Knotted Grove, the Battle of the Blackwyrm... I remember it all." This creature shares the word "wyrm" in its name; however, it is unclear whether it was a living Wyrm, a place or a moniker for something else.

At a certain point, all Wyrms died out or were wiped out from the world of Hallownest.Bardoon: "The Wyrm's corpse decaying. Endless. Rmm... Serene. Sad. With its like gone, the world is smaller."

The former body of the Pale King, the Cast-Off Shell similarly has a horned crown. The ash falling in Kingdom's Edge is moult that originates from this decaying corpse.Bardoon: "This falling ash is moult." At the very end of the Cast-Off Shell, the King's Brand can be acquired by the Knight, marking them as King.
