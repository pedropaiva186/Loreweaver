# The Cradle

The Cradle is at the peak of the Citadel that is able to be accessed after learning the Threefold Melody. Lace is found and fought on entrance, and after defeating her, Grand Mother Silk can be fought. The cradle is at the top of the map, and is the final area in the game accessed before act 3, if the requirements have been met.

The Pale Nails Silk Skill can be found here in act 3 after acquiring Silksoar, and the entrance to the Surface is here as well.
