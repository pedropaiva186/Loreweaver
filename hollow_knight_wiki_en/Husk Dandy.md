# Husk Dandy

Husk Dandies are enemies in Hollow Knight. Before dying and being reanimated by the Infection, these weak bugs used to be members of Hallownest's upper class.Wanderer's Journal, p. 73.Emilitia: "That's them outside, their bodies shambling around all mindless and empty. And I'm still alive to witness their pathetic demise."

Behaviour and Tactics
Meanders back and forth until engaged. Upon drawing close, it lunges towards the Knight and attacks with a flurry of unarmed blows. This attack is very short-ranged and leaves it open for a counterattack, as it is completely stationary while using it.

Location
Located mainly in the eastern portion of the City of Tears.

Trivia
