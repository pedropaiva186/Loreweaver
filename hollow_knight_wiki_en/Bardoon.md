# Bardoon

Bardoon is an NPC in Hollow Knight. He provides lore on the Cast-Off Shell and the Infection.

Lore
Bardoon is an old caterpillar found sleeping in Kingdom's Edge. He climbed up to the top of Kingdom’s Edge to get away from bugs plagued by the Infection.Bardoon: "For quiet retreat did I climb up here, away from spitting creatures. Ormmph... Yes. High up. Away from simple minds, lost to light." He resists the light’s allure, which he explains offers unity but also reduces a bug to instinct alone.Bardoon: "I resist the light's allure. Union it may offer, but also a mind bereft of thought... To instinct alone a bug is reduced..."

Bardoon has knowledge of the Pale King's origins and the King's Brand.Bardoon: "This ashen place is grave of Wyrm. Once told, it came to die. But what is death for that ancient being? More transformation methinks. This failed kingdom is product of the being spawned from that event." Though he knows about Wyrms, he is not one himself.Bardoon: "I am not a wyrm... Ohrm... Too small, I am. Too many limbs. No foresight like those old things."

In-game events
His body can be seen in the background, twisting through the cliff face. Bardoon's tail is found southeast of his head; hitting it makes him chuckle. He does not mind it when his tail is struck.Bardoon: "Is it you bothering about my tail? Rest is somewhat disturbed, but I find that sensation not unpleasant.."

Bardoon has new dialogue after the King's Brand has been acquired, as well as Void Heart.

Location
Bardoon's head can be found in the north part of Kingdom's Edge. His tail is found near the Lifeblood Cocoon in this area, behind a breakable wall.

Trivia
 Bardoon is one of the few NPCs who can be "hit" with the Nail without taking damage.
 Bardoon is one of the few NPCs who is able to detect when they are being Dream Nailed, along with Moths, Snail Shamans and the White Lady.
 Concept art of Bardoon labels him as "Deepnest Caterpillar", indicating that he was originally intended to be located in Deepnest.Edge Magazine March 2019 edition, p. 96.
 Kingdom's Edge used to be part of Deepnest. Deepnest was split in two after the removal of the Forest of Bones area. The east part was moved to the bottom right of the map and eventually became Kingdom's Edge. This can still be seen in Kingdom's Edge's file names, which start with "Deepnest_East".Team Cherry Mixer livestream.
