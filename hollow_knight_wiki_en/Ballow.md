# Ballow

Ballow is an  NPC in Hollow Knight: Silksong who is located in Deep Docks.

He is the assistant of Forge-DaughterHollow Knight: Silksong Gameplay - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2019 and can be found shovelling coal into a metal carapace. He can be hit by Hornet, which deals no damage to him and makes Forge-Daughter laugh.

Location
Ballow is located in the same room as Forge-Daughter in Deep Docks.
