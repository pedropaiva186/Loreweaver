# Relic Seeker Scrounge

Relic Seeker Scrounge is found in Bellhart village after you defeat Widow. 

First Encounter: What? What, what? Who is it? What've you got? What do you want?

Hornet: I am just a traveller, one hoping you were not too badly affected by the cursed snare set upon this down.

Scrounge: Pah! What binds them outside means nothing to me. None of them have my keen sense, my respect for the past!

You seem able, though. Sneaky, too. If you find any fine old stuff, anything with a history to it, bring it here.  I'll take it.

If you have a relic: But what's this?! I smell something on you right now! You have a relic for me!

Upon giving them the relic: Yes... this fits my collection well. Look, then! Look at what I have!

After looking at relics: More, then! Bring me more relics of the past! Records, seals, idols! Let's gather up the history of the world and hoard it all here, nice and safe! 

Hornet: If I happen upon more curiosities of this kingdom's past, I may visit again. Until that time, keep yourself safe.

Exhausted Dialogue: It's my calling it is, to gather the relics of the past. Look at my collection, if you fancy! Look, look!
