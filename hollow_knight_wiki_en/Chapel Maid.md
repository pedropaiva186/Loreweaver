# Chapel Maid

Chapel Maid is an NPC in Hollow Knight: Silksong. They appear to be carrying a large woodwind instrument.

Hornet meets the Chapel Maid just as she makes it out of the Moss Grotto from the Old Church and collapses. They act as a mentor of sorts throughout her journey in Pharloom.Hollow Knight: Silksong Gameplay - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2019 In the trailer, they are seen singing while Hornet plays her Needle.Hollow Knight: Silksong Reveal Trailer

In the Moss Grotto demo, they witness Hornet falling unconscious on the ground and start laughing, right before the end of the demo.
