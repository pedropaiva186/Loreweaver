# Magnetite dice

Playthings carved from magnetic material

Used in simple games and believed to bring luck to the bearer.

found by playing the dice game with Lumble the lucky found in the Blasted steps, near the bell way station, can also be found beside his corpse if encountered in later acts.

it gives the user a slight chance to not take damage

A general tip to win the game of dice is to listen for the pitch and throw the dice when the pitch is high.
