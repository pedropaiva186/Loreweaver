# Quests

Quests (or tasks) are a system in Hollow Knight: Silksong that Hornet may accept from NPCs found across Pharloom. Ongoing Quests appear on notice boards that may be found in various towns. 

These Quests are divided into categories:

 Donate: Pay a toll, usually shell shards or Rosaries.
 Gather: Find and collect materials.
 Wayfarer: Related to locating or Helping a lost NPC.
 Learn: Gather information about the  world.
 Hunt: Killing a specific enemy or group of enemies or some other form of combat trial.
 Grand Hunt: killing a boss or a mini-boss .
 Sprint: Win a race against Sprintmaster Swift
 Witness: Find Mister Mushroom in various locations across Pharloom
