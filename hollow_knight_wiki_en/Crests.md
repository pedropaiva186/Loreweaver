# Crests

Crests are important items in Hollow Knight: Silksong. There are 7 different crests, each of which entirely changes Hornet's moveset and which trinkets you can equip. The amount of trinkets on each crest can be increased by using Memory Lockets found throughout Pharloom. Each trinket has a corresponding color, which is visible on the crest. Most crests are located inside various dungeon-like areas with names corresponding to the crest within, for example Chapel of the Reaper. 

 Hunter Crest - 1 silk skill, 2 yellow, 2 red, 2 blue 

 "Stalk prey and attack with lethal strikes." 
The Hunter is the first crest obtained in Silksong, being equipped at the start of the game with 1 of each tool type available. It attacks in curved arcs, with the downslash causing the player to move diagonally down in the direction they are facing, this combines well with her dash attack which bounce her up in air, charged attack is a double attack.  In Weavenest Atla, the crest can be upgraded to gain a combo ability, successive attacks build up a meter, upon filling it, gain damage boost, this boost can stack upon filling an additional mete, getting hit reset all the meter. 

 Reaper Crest - 1 silk skill, 2 yellow, 2 red, 2 blue 

 "Attack with heavy, arcing slashes and reap additional Silk from enemies." 
The Reaper crest is a crest obtainable from the Chapel of the Reaper, it is a more defensive crest. It is distinct by its heavy slow attacks and its ability to gain additional silk from enemies upon hitting them after binding. The effect ends approximately 10 seconds after binding or when Hornet gets hit, the silk appears as white balls that spring from the enemy opposite from the direction it was hit from. every hit gives 1-3 balls of silk, 3 balls of silk gives one silk thread on the spool, each ball last for around 10 seconds. Its attacks alternates, starting with upper half slash, then lower half slash, up attack is also a half slash, downslash has a big arc which allows easy pogo maneuver, dash attack is a uppercut slash and charged attack is a big arc covering Hornet. The attacks do the same damage per strike as other crests, but with its slightly slower attack speed the Reaper crest does slightly less DPS overall. However, the wide swings have slightly more range than the Hunter attacks and they have more knockback, allowing Hornet to keep enemies at a safer distance while attacking to avoid damage.

 Wanderer Crest - 1 silk skill, 3 yellow, 1 red, 2 blue 

 "Cut swiftly and nimbly with short, precise strikes." 
The wanderer Crest can be obtained from the Chapel of the Wanderer (a sub area of Moss-Grotto), it allows you to hit  enemies in quick succession, it downward slash is an arc similar to reaper, allowing easier pogo movement, it has one dash attack, but hitting an enemy allows the follow up of an dive attack before using the downward slash, charged attack is a flurry of pierce attack (similar to Lace's parry attack). When having enough silk to bind, the crest will expand with silk appearing like a harp, while in this state, attacks have a chance of dealing critical damage (estimated to be triple, with an estimated chance of 5%).

 Beast Crest - 1 silk skill, 2 yellow, 2 red, 0 blue 

 "Tear foes with savage slashes. Bind to enter a wild fury and steal life from prey." 
The Beast Crest is Obtainable from the Chapel of the Beast in Hunter's March. It allows Hornet to strike with shorter-ranged slashes in the shape of claw marks. Binding grants Hornet a Berserker mode which grants lifesteal on hit, increased damage and attack speed for a short amount of time where every hit gives 1 mask, getting hit does not end the berseker state. This Berserker mode has the highest raw DPS rate of any crest related to using standard attacks. Note that downward attack follow a unique arc  that roll forward before rolling down, dash attack is a pounce slash.

Trivia: using this crest affects Hornet's taunt

 Witch Crest - 1 silk skill, 0 yellow, 2 red, 3 blue 

 "Whip the needle in sweeping swings. Leech the life of foes through twisted roots." 
Obtained after completing the Task Infestation Operation. Bind doesn't direct heal hornet instead create surrounding roots that damage and leech health, when binding if the roots kills the enemy beforehand, landing fewer hits, it heals less  masks. Injector Band and Multibinder affect to roots active duration. All attacks are slim arcs, downslash attack  is a down diagonal arc which makes Hornet weaves in and out of enemies, can perform two succesive dash attack, with the second one lifting Hornet up, charged attack is a whirl attack that can be extended through repeated attack command 

 Shaman Crest - 3 silk skills, 0 yellow, 0 red, 2 blue 

 "Cast the blade forward and empower Silk Skills with powerful runes." 
Increase silkskills damage. Its attacks creates shock wave, with charged creating a bigger shockwave, dash attack makes hornet jump and shoot a whrilblade. The Shaman forces you into the ground if you start healing in the air after a very short time.

 Architect Crest - 0 silk skills, 2 yellow, 3 red, 2 blue 

 "Spin the needle as a screw, shredding foes. Spend Silk to rapidly craft new tools." 
The Architect Crest is obtainable in the Chapel of the Architect in the Underworks, the Chapel needs the Key of the Architect to be unlocked, which can be  bought from the Twelfth Architect after acquiring 25 tools. The crest is distinct by it's Drill-like attacks and 2 variations of down-slashes where if the attack button is pressed once it initiates a short diagonal downslash while if it's held it initiates a slightly longer down-slash with a charge up, its charged attack is a massive drill attack, holding up when pressing bind to start craft bind, which doesn't heal hornet but craft to refill the tool, using up shards as normal crafting would do, craft bind is not affected by tools like Warding Bell, Injector Band or Multibinder.
