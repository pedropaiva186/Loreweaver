# Greymoor

On the way up to the Citadel at Pharloom's crest are the wasted caverns of Greymoor. The misted fields and dilapidated towers are now infested with Crawbugs, devious nesting types, and the former tenders of the land have become haunted.Blog post of June 25th, 2019.

Greymoor is described as a "huge" area, and one of the largest Team Cherry has ever created.
