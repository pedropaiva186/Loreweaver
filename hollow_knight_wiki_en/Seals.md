# Seals

centre|200px
Seals are magical formations which appear in both Hallownest and Pharloom. They have various functions and features.

Characteristics
Appearance
thumb|A typical seal's appearance|150px
Seals are white-glowing formations which consist of interconnected lines and geometric shapes. Some seals contain stylised imagery of certain characters such as the Hollow Knight. Most seals do not automatically appear but require certain actions before they are revealed, such as attacking an object or a character.

Function
Several seals are shown to protect living beings or inanimate objects from damage. Seals might also exist to block access to certain areas or to trap somebody in a specific place. The only named seal, the  Seal of Binding, is used to contain a powerful force or preserve something of great importance.Seal of Binding Hunter's Journal entry: "Intricate seal of ancient design. Used to contain a powerful force, or to preserve something of great importance." In some cases it is unknown what the function of a specific seal is.

History
Hallownest
thumb|200px|Black Egg entrance
Seals play a major role in Hallownest's history. The seals of the Dreamers were part of the Pale King's plan to seal the Radiance away. Three powerful bugs were chosen to enter an eternal sleep.Watcher's Spire lore tablet: "Sleep beckons eternal and these words become my last." They became seals on the entrance to the Black Egg to protect the Hollow Knight inside.Quirrel: "To save Hallownest, the Teacher willingly became a seal, but upon herself she enacted an additional protection."Resting Grounds lore tablet: "To protect the Vessel, the Dreamers lay sleeping."

When the Infection returned and the Hollow Knight weakened, the Dreamers' seals still remained.Dreamers: "Would the Seals break? They cannot be undone. But the Vessel weakens. That plague springs anew. They must be undone. Yet still our Seals remain. Our duty holds." Their seals can only be broken if their minds are accessed and they are killed inside their dream.Quirrel: "Ahh, but her mind is still locked to you. That final, powerful protection stays in place. If you would attempt to break her seal, you've first to discover the means to open a mind."

Pharloom
Hornet was taken to Pharloom in a cage by bugs of the Citadel's caste.Seth riddle description: "Many of the Citadel's caste have fallen to this deadly warrior." This cage was protected by a seal.Hollow Knight: Silksong Gameplay - Nintendo Treehouse: Live | E3 2019 At some point in the journey, a white flying bug appeared which landed on the cage and broke the seal. This eventually led to Hornet's escape into Moss Grotto.

Seal of Binding
right|75px
The Seal of Binding is an intricate seal of ancient design and the only named seal in Hallownest. Its main distinctive feature is the stylised head of the Hollow Knight at the top of the seal. In its Hunter's Journal entry, this seal is described as being used to contain a powerful force, or to preserve something of great importance.
 Path of PainThe full version of the Seal of Binding is shown at the entrance to the Path of Pain. The Hunter's Journal entry of the Seal of Binding is granted at the end of the Path of Pain.200px|thumb
