# Eva

Eva is a npc found in Weavenest Atla at Moss Grotto, she is capable of  upgrading Hornet's crest system when certain requirements are met.

The requirement is the number of unlocked slots on your cresst excluding the slots of Hunter's crest, an unlocked slot is any slot you can fit a tool or a silk skill. Any slot that is already unlocked when you adquired a crest counts toward the requirement.
