# Deepling

Deeplings are enemies in Hollow Knight. They are infants who creep around in the shadows, learning the layout of their home.Wanderer's Journal, p. 91.Deepling Hunter's Journal entry: "Infant of the Deepnest. Scuttles about in the darkness, learning the layout of its home."

Behaviour and Tactics
Skitters about, dealing contact damage. They are very weak but very quick. Be careful not to jump onto them while platforming.

Location
They are very commonly spotted in the west portion of Deepnest.

Trivia
 They used to sometimes spawn if one of the brown eggs found around Deepnest was broken.YouTube video showing a Deepling spawn from an egg. This was removed in the  Lifeblood update.
