# Shrumeling

Shrumelings are enemies in Hollow Knight. They are defenceless young fungal creatures who spend most of their time in the ground, blindly running away when threatened.Wanderer's Journal, p. 55.Shrumeling Hunter's Journal entry: "Young fungal creature. Spends most of its time in the ground [...]."

Behaviour and Tactics
Stationary, but flees once approached. Does not have any attacks, deals no contact damage and dies in one hit. A useful source of free SOUL.

Location
Shrumelings can be found in the Fungal Wastes.
